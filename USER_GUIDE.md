## 1. What the Website Does

Your new site at **gzci.ca** serves two main purposes:

1. **Public Showcase:** A clean, professional website highlighting Ground Zero's heavy civil, demolition, and remediation capabilities for prospective clients.
2. **Private Operations & Client Portal:** A secure portal where you can:
   - Create and organize client projects.
   - Track daily production quantities (e.g., cubic metres moved, tonnes crushed, square footage cleared).
   - See automatic pace calculations (ahead / behind schedule, projected completion dates).
   - **Upload and store project files (Dropbox):** Photos, site reports, daily notes, PDFs, invoices, and contracts.
   - Give clients their own private login where they see **only their project files and progress**—nothing else on the site.

## 3. How to Use the Portal (Step-by-Step)

### A. Logging In
1. Go to **[gzci.ca](https://gzci.ca)** on your computer, tablet, or phone.
2. Click the orange **`Staff / Client Login`** button in the top right.
3. Enter your email and password above.

---

### B. Creating a New Project
1. From your main dashboard, click **`+ New Project`**.
2. Follow the simple 4-step wizard:
   - **Step 1: Project Info:** Enter project name, client name, planned total units (e.g., `15,000` `m³`), start date, and target completion date.
   - **Step 2: Client Contacts & Logins:** Add your client contact (Name & Email). Assign them a simple password. The system creates their secure account automatically!
   - **Step 3: File Folders:** Pick which folders you want for this project (e.g., *Site Photos*, *Invoices*, *Soil Reports*, *Daily Notes*).
   - **Step 4: Review & Submit:** Confirm the details and launch the project.

---

### C. Logging Daily Production
1. Click on any project from your dashboard.
2. Under **Log Production Entry**:
   - Choose the date.
   - Enter the quantity completed that day.
   - Click **Log Entry**.
3. The dashboard automatically calculates:
   - Total completed vs. planned (% complete progress bar).
   - Actual daily pace vs. required daily pace to hit deadline.
   - Live projected finish date (shows if you are *Ahead*, *On Track*, or *Behind*).

---

### D. Using the File Dropbox (Photos, PDFs & Notes)
Scroll down to the **📁 Files & Documents** section on any project page:

1. **Upload Files:**  
   - Drag and drop files directly onto the box, or click **`+ Upload Files`**.
   - Accepts photos (JPG/PNG), PDFs, Word docs, Excel sheets, and videos up to 50MB each.
2. **Organize by Folder:**  
   - Click between the folder tabs (*General*, *Photos*, *Invoices*, *Daily Notes*, etc.) to keep files neat.
3. **View & Download:**  
   - Click **View** to instantly preview photos or PDFs right in your browser.
   - Click **Download** to save the file to your device.
4. **Delete Files:**  
   - Peter and Hanna can delete files with the red **Delete** button.

### E. How Client Access Works
- Clients log in with the email & password you created for them in Step 2.
- **Privacy Guaranteed:** Clients only see the specific project they are assigned to. They cannot see other clients, staff passwords, or unrelated projects.
- Clients can view their project's progress and upload/download documents in their designated folders.

### F. Job Costing — Bid vs Actual (Peter's daily number)

Every project now has a green **💰 Bid vs Actual** button at the top. It has 4 tabs:

1. **👀 Where am I? (Peter — start here every day).** One big banner tells you if you're
   **WINNING or LOSING** and by how much. Below it: what you bid, what you've spent,
   how far along the job is (%), a Mon–Fri day-by-day spending list, and one card per
   part of the job (green = under budget, red = over). Tap the progress buttons
   (Not started / ¼ / ½ / ¾ / Done) as work finishes — the winning/losing number
   updates instantly.
2. **📝 What we bid.** One line per thing (crew, trucks, fuel, dirt in/out): what 1 costs
   us and what we charge the customer for 1. Use **"Start with the normal job list"**
   to pre-fill a standard 10-line template, then change the numbers.
3. **🧾 Log spending (Mike / Hanna, Mon–Fri — Peter skips this).** Log what got spent
   each day: either quantity × price-each (400 litres × $1.72) or a flat receipt total
   ($84 lunch), plus hours for labour and a receipt note. The Where-am-I screen
   updates by itself.
4. **📂 Papers.** Bid documents, the signed contract, and invoices/receipts live next
   to the numbers. Snap a receipt photo and upload it here.

**📤 Sending the update:** on the Where-am-I screen, the "Send this update" panel writes
the whole week up in plain words (bid, spent, % done, Mon–Fri totals, over/under budget
lines) with a live preview. One tap to **Copy**, **Email**, **Text**, **Print**, or
**save the spreadsheet (CSV)** for Excel.

## 5. Role Playbooks — Who Does What

### MIKE (Project Manager — field truth, Mon–Fri)

You own everything that happens on site, in the app that means: log it, keep the bid current, move the %s. The one thing you can't do is delete — that protects everyone from accidents.

**Daily logging — two ways, your choice:**
- **A. In the app (best when you have signal).** Log spending tab → pick the bid line (unit + usual rate fill in) → type how many/hours → Log ✓. 30 seconds a line.
- **B. Drop the filled sheet (best from the laptop).** Tap **📥 Download blank daily sheet** once — it's pre-filled with every bid line. Fill it during the day in Excel, then drag-drop the `.csv` back onto the Log tab. You'll get a preview: green rows log as-is, red rows need a fix (bad date, unknown line name — pick the right bid line from the dropdown). Nothing enters the books until you hit confirm.
- Either way: same-day entries, one line per cost event, receipt photo in **📂 Papers → Invoices & receipts** the same day.
- Watch the **📝 "Today still open" strip** at the top of the Log tab — it lists bid lines with nothing logged for that day. Tap one to start its entry. When the strip turns green ("all logged"), the day is closed and Friday will have no holes. That's the whole Tank-210 lesson in one box.
- No signal? The filled sheet *is* the backup — import it the next morning.

**Keeping the job current:** the bid is yours — you wrote it. **✏️ Change** any line when scope shifts, move the **% finished** buttons as work completes. Mistake you can't undo? Hanna or Peter deletes; ask them.

**Two logs, never mixed:** **📊 Daily Quantity Log** (project page) = amounts of work (loads, days). **🧾 Log spending** = dollars. Same event, two different tabs, never the same entry twice.

### HANNA (Office — payroll, bookkeeping, true costs)

You're not onsite, so you never audit Mike's entries — what he logged is what happened. Your jobs:

1. **True costs (one-time per job, most important job on this list).** Open **📝 What we bid**. Every line flagged **⚠️ True cost needed** still shows the charge-out rate where the real cost belongs. Tap **✏️ Change** on each line and type what GZC actually pays (wages, rental rates, fuel cost). The amber ⏳ ESTIMATE banner disappears as you clear them — when it's gone, Peter's numbers are real.
2. **Fixes on request.** Mike can't delete — if he flags a duplicate or typo, you (or Peter) remove it. You don't patrol his entries; you correct what he reports.
3. **Papers.** Supplier invoices go in **📂 Papers → Invoices & receipts**; signed extras and contracts in their folders.
4. **Friday books (15 min).** Open **👀 Where am I?**: Mon–Fri list complete. Then use the **📤 Payroll & books** panel: set the week range and download exactly what each system needs —
   - **🟢 Sage 50 timesheet** → Sage 50 → File → Import/Export → Import Records → Timesheets. One row per person per day, Regular vs Overtime split, job name attached. Worker names must already exist in Sage with identical spelling; confirm the Income names "Regular"/"Overtime" match payroll's setup.
   - **🔵 QB hours** → QBO weekly timesheet (or Transaction Pro / QB Time import): Employee, Date, Pay Type, Hours, Job.
   - **🧾 QB expenses** → Banking → Upload from file, or straight to the accountant: every dated cost line with category, receipt ref, and worker.
   - Any hour entry missing a worker name is flagged before you download — Sage rejects nameless rows, so add names on the Log tab first.
5. **Payroll prep (current method).** Until the named-worker roster lands, use the CSV's Hours column plus Mike's descriptions to attribute reg/OT. Keep the naming habit consistent with Mike now ("6 labourers × 8 reg + 2 OT") — it becomes roster data later.

### PETER (Owner — 5 minutes a day, 15 on Friday)

You read. You don't enter data.

1. **Daily glance.** Open the job → **💰 Bid vs Actual** → **👀 Where am I?** Read exactly one thing: the big banner. Green winning, red losing, amber means Hanna still has true costs to enter — call her, not the site.
2. **Friday ritual (this is the whole system working).**
   - Check the Mon–Fri day list: 5 days of entries? If a day is empty, ask Mike what happened.
   - Tap each job part to its honest % (Not started / ¼ / ½ / ¾ / Done). The winning/losing number updates instantly.
   - Tap **📋 Copy the words** or **✉️ Email it** — the update writes itself. Send it to whoever needs it.
3. **What the colors mean.** Green "Under budget 👍" = spending less than bid. Red "Over budget ⚠️" = spending more — tap the line, read what drove it, decide tomorrow's plan differently. That's the firewall conversation from the trailer, now with numbers.
4. **Change requests.** Never edit numbers yourself in a hurry — tell Hanna what changed ("bin truck is now 45 days, not 40") and she updates the bid so history stays clean.
5. **Clients.** They can open the job and see progress % and crew-on-site days only. No dollars ever reach them — that's automatic, nothing for you to set.
